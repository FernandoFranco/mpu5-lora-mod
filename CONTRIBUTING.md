# Contributing

This repository is **read-only** for the community: it **does not accept Pull Requests**. Any PR opened by third parties will be closed without review.

That doesn't mean your help isn't welcome. Suggestions and bug reports go in as **Issues**:

- **Fit or printing problems:** open an [Issue](https://github.com/FernandoFranco/mpu5-lora-mod/issues/new) describing the part, your printer and material, and attach a photo if possible.
- **Suggestions and ideas:** open an Issue explaining what you would like to see in the project.
- **Remixes:** feel free to adapt the files for other replicas and boards, as long as you respect the license (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). If you want, tell us about your remix in an Issue.
- **Field photos:** show off your build on social media, linking the project or tagging **@franco.da.hk416**.

## Before opening an Issue

- Search existing Issues to avoid duplicates.
- Be concise and include as much context as you can: board version, Meshtastic firmware, the part involved.

## Commercial use

Selling parts, kits or services based on this project requires prior authorization. To request it, open an Issue.
